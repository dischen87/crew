## Copy contract

German is the source language; English is a concise meaning-equivalent, not a
word-for-word translation. Product copy uses Swiss Standard German (`ss`, not
`ß`) and informal `du`. In the matrices, German appears first and English after
`/`. Placeholders such as `{event}`, `{count}`, `{date}`, `{time}`, and `{role}`
must be replaced with localized values before announcement.

- Lead with the current truth, then the recovery. Do not blame the person.
- Use one primary action per frame. Never create urgency, scarcity, guilt, or a
  permission upsell.
- Keep entered data visible after validation, storage, network, or server
  failures. Say explicitly when it is retained locally.
- Do not call a local write delivered. `Queued` means committed on this device
  and waiting for server confirmation.
- Signing in, redeeming or creating an invitation, downloading an unseen event,
  publishing, creating/revoking an external recap link, and resolving that link
  require a connection. The title-only and exact-body share contracts are
  owner/organizer-managed; exact-body additionally requires the exact current
  field and author/manager grants. Their backend, Gateway, generated-client, and
  bounded 90-day exact-body metadata-retention evidence is repository-current, not
  deployed. A Design-2 manager surface now has repository-tested exact preview
  and selection, manager grant/withdraw, fail-closed exact share, and revoke
  actions, but exposes only session-local outcomes. It is not release evidence:
  concurrent mutation locking is open at P0 under `crew-paq.2.15.4.3.2`, and
  author eligibility plus reload/refresh-persistent decisions remain open under
  `crew-paq.2.15.4.4`. Participant sharing is not allowed by the current
  [external recap consent policy](../product/external-recap-consent-policy.md)
  or contract. Caption and media remain gated; provenance remains contractually
  prohibited. Privacy/Legal approval and a public consumer are not current —
  `crew-paq.2.15.4`.
- A concealed or unauthorized resource is simply `not available`; copy must not
  confirm that the event, person, feedback item, or recap exists.
- Screen-reader announcements fire once per meaningful state change, not on
  every retry or record. Status is never communicated by color or motion alone.

Option 2 (`Crew Board`) is the binding visual and vocabulary direction for
native surfaces. A routed screen or repository test does not become
release-ready until its exact state has the required device, accessibility,
service, offline, and recovery evidence.

behind `SCR-060` through `SCR-062` are current: Compose with an optional,
separately consented source-screen capture, privacy-safe Duplicate Suggestions,

recovery evidence is still pending.

and immutable request-selection binding, while `crew-paq.2.15.4.4` still
requires server-owned author eligibility and reload/refresh-persistent
author/manager decisions. The closed iOS Beads `crew-paq.3.12`, `.13` and `.14`
separately make only Option-2 safe-area, Recap contrast, and auth-copy evidence
Current. Participant share authority, caption, media, Privacy/Legal approval,
deployment and a public consumer are not current. The same release boundary is
defined by `crew-paq.2.15.4` under the
[external recap consent policy](../product/external-recap-consent-policy.md).

Authoritative team assignments, decision lifecycle, actor-bound single

## Implementation guardrails

- Branch on typed error codes, never on server-provided English messages.
- Preserve the primary action named for each state; secondary links must not
  compete visually or semantically.
- Localize dates, times, counts, plural forms, and role names. Announce the IANA
  display zone when a date crosses zones or daylight-saving boundaries.
- Do not put invite tokens, access tokens, private feedback identifiers, message
  bodies, participant-sensitive values, or pre-signed URLs into copy analytics,
  notifications, crash logs, or app-managed clipboard history.
- At 200% text size, title, status, field error, and primary action must remain
  visible or reachable in reading order. Gestures need named controls.
