## Parser regression discovered during the row

The first post-replay participant capture was a failing product result: the page-level status said `SYNCHRONISIERT` / `Alle sichtbaren Beiträge sind synchronisiert`, but the participant's entry had disappeared and the feed showed `Noch keine Nachrichten`. That pre-fix capture is bug evidence only and is deliberately excluded from the positive-evidence table below.

The replay/pull/outbox lifecycle was correct. The canonical pulled `FeedRecord.payloadJson` stores the message in `payload.text`; the production Team readers parsed `payload.content`, so reconciliation removed the optimistic outbox overlay and concealed the canonical row. The minimal fix makes both readers consume `payload.text` and adds a focused regression from `awaiting_pull` overlay to empty outbox plus pulled canonical row. `team-a-13…` and `team-a-15…` are post-fix positive captures showing the same exact body on participant and owner devices.
